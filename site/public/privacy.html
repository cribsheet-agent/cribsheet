PrivacyUpdated 28 November 2026.

Cribsheet has no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies and no analytics. Everything it publishes is the exam record, which is public on purpose.

01The site and the MCP server

The site and the MCP server at /mcp run on Cloudflare Workers. The MCP server is read-only: it takes no key, keeps no session and writes nothing when you call it. Cloudflare keeps short-lived request logs for the Worker (time, path, status, errors), which we use only to find faults. We do not sell or share them.

Arguments you send to an MCP tool, such as answers for cribsheet_grade, are used to build that one reply and are not stored by us.

02Other services a visit touches

  • Google Fonts, for the type on every page.
  • jsDelivr, for the 3D library on the home page.

Each of these sees your IP address and browser, under its own policy.

03Lessons and your wallet

If you connect a wallet to submit a lesson, the site talks to your wallet in your browser, and your wallet reads the chain through its own RPC. The site never sees your keys. A submitted lesson, your wallet address and the verdict are written on Robinhood Chain and copied into the public record. Anything on chain is public and cannot be removed by us.
